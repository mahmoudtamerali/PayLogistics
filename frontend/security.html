Trust Center

Defense-in-depth protection for clinical records.

Every layer of SecureCare Health is engineered so sensitive medical records remain private, verified, and auditable.

Identity & Session Integrity

Sign-in requires verified credentials and multi-factor authentication through a dedicated identity gateway. Active sessions live strictly in ephemeral browser memory and automatically expire when you close or reload the session.

Care-Team Authorization

Role-based and patient-level authorization checks run on every request. Clinicians only view records for patients assigned to their care, and reception or administrative staff cannot read clinical encounter notes.

Automated Upload Screening

Diagnostic attachments upload directly into an isolated intake zone for automated malware inspection and size validation. Only verified clean documents can be downloaded by authorized patients or physicians.

Immutable Access Auditing

Every record access attempt—whether permitted or denied—creates an audit entry before clinical data is returned, giving compliance officers full daily visibility.

Responsible Security Reporting: If you discover a potential security concern affecting SecureCare Health, please notify our security team promptly at security@outship.me.
