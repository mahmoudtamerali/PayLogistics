1. Not for Medical Emergencies

Emergency Notice: SecureCare Health is not intended for urgent or life-threatening medical conditions. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency hospital department immediately.

2. Account Security & Eligibility

You are responsible for safeguarding your sign-in credentials and multi-factor authentication device. Accounts are strictly personal and non-transferable. Notify clinic support immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

3. Appointments & Diagnostic Uploads

Appointments booked through the portal are subject to clinician availability. Please cancel or reschedule visits promptly if you cannot attend. Diagnostic uploads must be legible PDF, PNG, or JPEG documents (up to 10 MB) belonging to the designated patient; files flagged during automated screening are quarantined and will not be opened by clinical staff.

4. Acceptable Use & Auditability

Attempting to view records outside your authorized care relationship, submitting harmful files, or interfering with portal availability is strictly prohibited. All record access events are logged for patient protection and regulatory compliance.
