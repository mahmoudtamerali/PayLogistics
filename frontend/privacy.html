Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Notice & Patient Data Rights

Effective November 2026 · Explains how personal and medical information is handled within SecureCare Health.

Contents
1. Information We Process 2. Clinical Confidentiality 3. Your Data Rights 4. Retention & Safeguards 5. Privacy Officer Contact

1. Information We Process

When you register or receive care through SecureCare Health, we process only the categories of personal and clinical data needed to deliver healthcare services:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full legal name, email address, and telephone number.
  • Appointment Records: Scheduled dates, visit types (consultation, follow-up, laboratory visit), attending clinician, and visit status.
  • Clinical Encounter Notes: Physician observations and visit summaries authored by clinicians assigned to your care.
  • Laboratory & Diagnostic Files: Patient- or lab-submitted PDF reports and medical images, alongside file screening verdicts.

2. Clinical Confidentiality & Access Boundaries

Access to records is strictly governed by clinical role and explicit care-team assignment:

  • Patients may view only their own profile, appointments, visit notes, and verified laboratory files.
  • Attending Physicians may review clinical notes and diagnostic documents solely for patients actively assigned to their care.
  • Clinic & Lab Staff manage appointment scheduling and laboratory intake uploads, and are barred from reading clinical visit notes.
  • Compliance Administrators manage clinician assignments and review daily access audit trails without reading clinical note content.

3. Your Data Rights (Access, Portability & Erasure)

Authenticated patients can exercise their privacy rights directly inside the portal under Profile & Data Rights:

  • Right to Rectification: Update your full name, email address, and phone number at any time.
  • Right of Access & Portability: Request and download a machine-readable JSON archive containing your profile, visits, and record metadata.
  • Right to Erasure: Submit a formal data erasure request with confirmation and reason, and track the status of your request directly in the portal.

4. Retention & Protection Safeguards

Health records are encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by mandatory multi-factor authentication, and logged upon every access. Unscanned or quarantined uploads are automatically isolated and never released for clinical download. Data retention aligns with applicable clinical record obligations and verified erasure workflows.

5. Privacy Officer Contact

For questions regarding your health records, consent withdrawal, or data protection inquiries, contact our Data Protection Office at privacy@outship.me.
